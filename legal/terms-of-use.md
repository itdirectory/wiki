### 2. Description of Services

IT Directory is a SaaS IT inventory and asset management platform used to manage hardware, employee directory data, software licenses and services, network inventory, domains, SSO and connector integrations, an employee portal, electronic equipment handover forms, reminders, reports, and related organizational metadata.

The Services are intended for IT professionals, administrators, technicians, and managed service providers, and for Portal Users to whom a Customer gives portal access. The Services are not an endpoint management, MDM, help desk, procurement, accounting, legal compliance, security monitoring, incident response, backup, records-archiving, or qualified electronic signature service unless expressly agreed in writing.

Features may include:

* Hardware inventory and assignment tracking.
* Employee directory management.
* Software license, SaaS, service, seat, owner, renewal, and cost tracking.
* Network inventory, including VLANs, subnets, gateways, DHCP configuration, locations, public IP allocations, and providers.
* Domain, DNS, WHOIS/RDAP, SSL/TLS, and subdomain monitoring, and recording the employees responsible for domains.
* Microsoft Entra ID SSO, Microsoft Entra ID employee sync, Microsoft Intune device sync, Microsoft 365 license sync, and Cloudflare sync.
* An employee portal with emailed sign-in codes, through which Portal Users view their assigned items, domains, and forms.
* Electronic equipment handover forms signed by Portal Users, and return receipts recorded by Customer users.
* Customer branding (logos) shown in the portal, on generated PDFs, and in branded emails.
* Bulk imports, reports (including cost, spending, renewal-forecast, and license-utilization reports), report emails, reminder emails, and administrative settings.
* Trial, billing status, asset limits, sub-organization limits, and entitlement management.
